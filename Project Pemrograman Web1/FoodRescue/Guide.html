Small Actions Big Impact

Practical and simple ways you can do to reduce food waste in your daily life

Smart Shopping

Buy only what you need, make a shopping list, and choose the right portions for your plans.

→

Proper Food Storage

Keep food fresh longer by storing it correctly, such as airtight containers and at the right temperature.

→

Portion Control

Serve a portion that fits your needs and avoid taking more food than you can finish.

→

Food Sharing

Share excess food that is still safe to eat with friends, neighbors, or food-sharing communities.

→

Composting

Turn food scraps, such as fruit and vegetable peels, into compost to reduce waste and enrich the soil.

→

Start with one simple habit today, and see the bigger impact tomorrow
